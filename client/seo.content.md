## 18. Don't overuse “SEO-optimized”

One thing I'd change from your current website copy:

> “Generate SEO-optimized titles, descriptions, and keywords…”

It's okay occasionally, but **“SEO-optimized” is becoming generic marketing language** in this niche. Competitors use it heavily. ([StockMetaPro][3])

I'd favor:

**“Relevant, stock-ready metadata”**

or

**“AI-generated titles, descriptions and keywords for stock content.”**

It sounds more credible.

Also avoid promising:

> “keywords that rank higher”

> “guaranteed sales”

> “more downloads”

> “guaranteed approvals”

Instead:

> **“Create relevant metadata that helps you prepare your content for stock marketplace search.”**

That is much safer and more credible.
